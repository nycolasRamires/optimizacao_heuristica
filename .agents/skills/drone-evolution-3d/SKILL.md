# 3D Quadrotor Evolution & Reinforcement Learning Guide

This skill provides the architectural foundation, equations of motion, RL training patterns, and genetic optimization workflows for high-throughput 3D quadrotor navigation and obstacle avoidance.

---

## 1. 6-DOF Quadrotor Rigid-Body Dynamics

Transitioning from 2D planar acceleration ($\ddot{\mathbf{p}} = \mathbf{a}$) to 3D flight requires full $SE(3)$ rigid-body kinematics and dynamics:

### Translational Kinematics & Newton's Second Law
$$\dot{\mathbf{p}} = \mathbf{v}, \quad m \dot{\mathbf{v}} = m \mathbf{g} + \mathbf{R}(\mathbf{q}) \mathbf{T}_B + \mathbf{F}_{\text{drag}}$$
where:
- $\mathbf{p} = [x, y, z]^T \in \mathbb{R}^3$ (world frame position)
- $\mathbf{v} = [v_x, v_y, v_z]^T \in \mathbb{R}^3$ (world frame linear velocity)
- $\mathbf{g} = [0, 0, -9.81]^T \text{ m/s}^2$ (gravity vector)
- $\mathbf{T}_B = [0, 0, T]^T$ with total thrust $T = \sum_{i=1}^4 T_i = \sum k_T \Omega_i^2$ along the body $z$-axis
- $\mathbf{R}(\mathbf{q}) \in SO(3)$ is the rotation matrix derived from unit attitude quaternion $\mathbf{q} = [q_w, q_x, q_y, q_z]^T$
- $\mathbf{F}_{\text{drag}} = -\text{diag}(d_x, d_y, d_z) \mathbf{v}$ (aerodynamic linear drag)

### Rotational Kinematics & Euler's Equations
$$\dot{\mathbf{q}} = \frac{1}{2} \mathbf{q} \otimes \begin{bmatrix} 0 \\ \boldsymbol{\omega}_B \end{bmatrix}$$
$$\mathbf{J} \dot{\boldsymbol{\omega}}_B = \boldsymbol{\tau}_B - \boldsymbol{\omega}_B \times (\mathbf{J} \boldsymbol{\omega}_B) + \boldsymbol{\tau}_{\text{gyro}}$$
where:
- $\boldsymbol{\omega}_B = [p, q, r]^T$ is the body angular rate vector
- $\mathbf{J} = \text{diag}(I_{xx}, I_{yy}, I_{zz})$ is the inertia tensor
- $\boldsymbol{\tau}_B = [\tau_\phi, \tau_\theta, \tau_\psi]^T$ are body torques generated by differential rotor thrusts and reaction drag torques

### Control Hierarchy: Direct Thrust vs. Cascaded Commands
For fast RL training, a **Cascaded Attitude Control Interface** is recommended over raw motor RPMs:
$$\mathbf{u}_{\text{RL}} = [T_{\text{norm}}, \phi_{\text{cmd}}, \theta_{\text{cmd}}, \dot{\psi}_{\text{cmd}}]^T \in [-1, 1]^4$$
- The RL policy commands thrust ratio and desired tilt angles $(\phi, \theta)$ plus yaw rate $\dot{\psi}$.
- An inner-loop PD/PID controller converts attitude errors into motor commands at $500\text{ Hz}$, while the RL policy operates at $50\text{–}100\text{ Hz}$. This preserves RL convergence while guaranteeing physical stability.

## 2. High-Throughput Vectorized 3D Perception

In 2D, 15 planar rays yielded $40{,}000+$ FPS. In 3D, perception must remain vectorized without scalar Python loops:

### Spherical Raycasting Grid
Define an array of $M$ azimuth angles $\psi_i \in [-\pi, \pi]$ and $K$ elevation rings $\phi_j \in [-\phi_{\max}, \phi_{\max}]$ ($N = M \times K$ rays):
$$\mathbf{d}_{i,j} = \mathbf{R}(\mathbf{q}) \begin{bmatrix} \cos \phi_j \cos \psi_i \\ \cos \phi_j \sin \psi_i \\ \sin \phi_j \end{bmatrix}$$

### 3D Primitive Intersections (NumPy Matrix Operations)
1. **Infinite Planes / Bounding Walls & Floors:**
   $$t = \frac{(\mathbf{p}_{\text{plane}} - \mathbf{p}_0) \cdot \mathbf{n}}{\mathbf{d} \cdot \mathbf{n}}$$
2. **3D Cylindrical Obstacles (Pillars):**
   Project ray and cylinder to 2D horizontal plane $(x, y)$, solve quadratic discriminant $A t^2 + B t + C = 0$, then verify vertical bound $z_{\min} \le z_0 + t d_z \le z_{\max}$.
3. **Oriented Bounding Boxes (OBBs):**
   Transform ray into the box's local coordinate frame using $\mathbf{R}_{\text{box}}^T$, then evaluate standard vectorized Slab intersection tests along $(x, y, z)$.

## 3. 3D Markov Observation Space ($\mathbb{R}^{D}$)

To satisfy the Markov property ($\mathbb{P}(s_{t+1}|s_t, a_t) = \mathbb{P}(s_{t+1}|s_0 \dots s_t, a_t)$), the agent must observe both inertial and navigational states:

| Component | Dimensions | Physical Meaning & Normalization |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **3D Relative Target Vector** | 3D ($[x, y, z]_{\text{local}}$) | Target position in body coordinates normalized by max arena diameter |
| **Target Distance & Bearing** | 2D ($d_{\text{norm}}, \theta_{\text{bearing}}$) | Direct Euclidean distance and horizontal heading error |
| **Linear Body Velocity** | 3D ($[u, v, w]_B$) | Longitudinal, lateral, and vertical velocity $/ v_{\max}$ |
| **Attitude Representation** | 4D ($\mathbf{q}$) or 6D (Rotation matrix columns) | Full 3D orientation avoiding Euler angle singularities |
| **Body Angular Velocity** | 3D ($[p, q, r]$) | Roll, pitch, and yaw rates $/ \omega_{\max}$ |
| **3D LiDAR Range Array** | $N$D (e.g. $16 \times 8 = 128$) | Normalized sensor distances $\in [0, 1]$ |

## 4. 3D Reward Shaping & The U-Trap Solution

Extend the 2D shaping formulations to 3D flight:

### Multi-Step 3D Retreat Tolerance (Resolving 3D U-Traps)
$$\Delta d_{\text{retreat}} = \|\mathbf{p}_t - \mathbf{p}_{\text{goal}}\| - \min_{k \in [t-K, t-1]} \|\mathbf{p}_k - \mathbf{p}_{\text{goal}}\|$$
$$P_{\text{retreat}} = \begin{cases} c_{\text{ret}} + k_{\text{ret}} (\Delta d_{\text{retreat}} - \delta_{\text{tol}}) & \text{if } \Delta d_{\text{retreat}} > \delta_{\text{tol}} \\ 0 & \text{otherwise} \end{cases}$$
- Allows climbing over or navigating around 3D obstacles without instantaneous penalty within slack margin $\delta_{\text{tol}} = 0.1\text{m}$.

### 3D Attitude & Stability Regularization
- **Tilt Penalty:** $P_{\text{tilt}} = c_{\text{tilt}} \cdot (1 - \hat{\mathbf{z}}_B \cdot \hat{\mathbf{z}}_W) = c_{\text{tilt}} \cdot (1 - \cos \theta_{\text{tilt}})$. Discourages excessive roll/pitch that leads to aerodynamic stalling.
- **Altitude Safety Buffer:** $P_{\text{floor}} = c_{\text{floor}} \cdot \mathbb{I}(z < z_{\text{min\_safe}})$ prevents ground-effect turbulences and premature crash landings.

## 5. Genetic Algorithm Multi-Tier Reproduction Standard

To preserve diversity and prevent premature genetic convergence across 30+ generations:

```
[Generation g Survivors] (SR >= Dynamic Viability Threshold)
          │
          ├───> Group 2: Elites (Top n_best = 4) ───> Direct Clones (No Retrain)
          │
          ├───> Group 3: Elite Crossover ───────────> C(n_best, 2) Elite × Elite ONLY (Mutated)
          │
          ├───> Group 4: Tournament Selection ──────> Non-Elite × Non-Elite ONLY (Mutated)
          │
          └───> Group 1: Dead Replacements ─────────> Offspring of Non-Elites / Immigrants
```

### Mandatory Invariants:
1. **Tier Separation:** Never pair an elite with a non-elite in tournament crossover.
2. **Mutation Integrity:** All non-elites must undergo probabilistic mutation ($p_{\text{mut}} = 0.20$) to ensure continuous diversity.
3. **Dynamic Viability Schedule:** Ramp survival criteria over generations ($50\% \to 80\%$ SR) to drive evolutionary progress.
4. **PyTorch Optimizer Override:** Always invoke `set_learning_rate(model, lr)` to update `param_groups['lr']` when fine-tuning warm-started checkpoints.
